By using a custom [DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) skin for DEX 3 you can dramatically change the overall look and feel of the software, even change, edit or add functionality not present in the default skin included with the installation. Our [free skin designer software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) simplifies the skin mapping process with visual tools; just create your design in a graphic design application such as Photoshop or Illustrator with up and down button states and drop into the skin designer to map images to features supported by DEX 3.
